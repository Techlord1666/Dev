Be the Voice. Be the Hope. Be the Change.

Real change happens when compassionate people take action. At DEVADI, every volunteer, donor, and partner plays a vital role in transforming lives and strengthening communities. Whether you are a lawyer, educator, healthcare professional, student, skilled professional, or simply passionate about making a difference, there is a place for you at DEVADI.

Volunteer With Us

Use your time and skills to create lasting impact.

Volunteers support our programs in:

  • Access to justice and community advocacy
  • Girls' education and youth mentorship
  • Hunger relief and food distribution
  • Women's empowerment initiatives
  • Prevention of gender based violence
  • Community outreach, research, media, and administration
Lawyers for Justice

Your legal expertise can restore hope.

We welcome lawyers, law students, and legal professionals who are passionate about expanding access to justice. Join us to provide:

  • Pro bono legal representation
  • Legal advice and rights awareness
  • Prison visits and inmate counseling
  • Case review and legal research
  • Advocacy for vulnerable and unjustly incarcerated persons

Your legal expertise can restore hope, protect rights, and help secure justice for those who have no voice.

Where Your Gift Goes

Every donation, put to work.

DEVADI operates with transparency and accountability to our beneficiaries and donors. Your contribution directly funds:

Food Security — nutritious meals for families facing hunger.

Education — scholarships and learning materials for girls and children.

Protection — survivor support and gender justice advocacy.

Together, We Can Make a Difference

Every act of kindness, every hour volunteered, every professional skill shared, and every donation brings us closer to a Nigeria where justice is accessible, hunger is reduced, girls are educated, women are empowered, and every person can live with dignity.

Join DEVADI today. Together, we can give hope to the vulnerable, amplify the voices of the unheard, and build stronger, more inclusive communities.
