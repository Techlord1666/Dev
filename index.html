Our Mission

Advancing equality through action.

DEVADI exists to provide immediate relief and long-term empowerment to the less privileged and marginalized individuals in our communities. Our goal is to address the root causes of poverty and inequality, ensuring that no one is left behind.

We work across five critical pillars — Food Security, Education, Youth Development, Women's Empowerment, and Gender Justice.

More about us
Areas of Focus

Five pillars, one commitment.

Food Security & Nutrition

Regular meals and nutritional support for the less privileged and indigent, ensuring food security and dignity for vulnerable families.

Education Access

Removing barriers to learning by supporting children's education, with emphasis on girls' education, scholarships, and safe learning spaces.

Youth Mentorship

Life skills training, vocational guidance, and personal development for young people to become leaders and productive members of society.

Women's Empowerment

Skills training, economic support, business development, and leadership programs for young women and single mothers.

Gender Justice & Advocacy

Awareness campaigns, survivor support, community engagement, and policy advocacy for a safer, more equal society.
