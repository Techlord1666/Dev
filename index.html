Our Mission

Our work is built around five core mission areas:

ACCESS TO JUSTICE

Justice should never depend on wealth, status, or influence. We provide legal education, legal aid, and advocacy for vulnerable people, including unjustly incarcerated individuals and underserved communities. By promoting human rights and strengthening access to justice, we help ensure that every voice is heard and every person is treated fairly under the law.

GIRLS AND CHILDREN EDUCATION

Every girl deserves the opportunity to learn, dream, and succeed. We work to remove barriers that prevent girls from accessing quality education by supporting school enrolment, learning opportunities, mentorship, and educational resources. We believe educating a girl transforms families, communities, and future generations.

HUNGER RELIEF AND FOOD SECURITY

No one should have to choose between hunger and survival. We provide emergency food assistance to vulnerable families while promoting sustainable food security initiatives that strengthen household resilience, restore dignity, and improve community wellbeing.

WOMEN’S EMPOWERMENT

Women are powerful agents of change. Through skills development, entrepreneurship support, leadership training, and economic empowerment programs, we equip women with the knowledge, confidence, and opportunities to become financially independent and improve the wellbeing of their families and communities.

PREVENTION OF GENDER BASED VIOLENCE

Every woman and girl has the right to live free from violence, abuse, and discrimination. We promote awareness, prevention, advocacy, and survivor centered support while engaging families and communities to challenge harmful practices and create safer environments where women and girls can thrive.

At DEVADI, we believe lasting change begins when people are empowered with knowledge, protected by justice, and supported with opportunities to build better lives. Through collaboration with communities, volunteers, donors, and development partners, we are restoring hope, protecting dignity, and creating pathways to a more just, inclusive, and prosperous Nigeria. Together, we can give a voice to the unheard, protect the vulnerable, and build communities where every person has the opportunity to thrive.
