About Us

Demain Voix Advocacy and Development Initiative (DEVADI)

is a Nigerian non governmental organization committed to advancing justice, protecting human rights, and empowering vulnerable individuals and communities to live with dignity, equality, and hope.

Across Nigeria, millions of people continue to face injustice, hunger, gender inequality, and limited access to education and opportunities. Children are forced out of school, women and girls experience violence and discrimination, families struggle to meet their basic needs, and many vulnerable people are unable to access justice. DEVADI exists to change this reality through practical, people centered solutions that restore hope and create lasting impact.

Our Mission

Our work is built around five core mission areas:

ACCESS TO JUSTICE

Justice should never depend on wealth, status, or influence. We provide legal education, legal aid, and advocacy for vulnerable people, including unjustly incarcerated individuals and underserved communities. By promoting human rights and strengthening access to justice, we help ensure that every voice is heard and every person is treated fairly under the law.

GIRLS AND CHILDREN EDUCATION

Every girl deserves the opportunity to learn, dream, and succeed. We work to remove barriers that prevent girls from accessing quality education by supporting school enrolment, learning opportunities, mentorship, and educational resources. We believe educating a girl transforms families, communities, and future generations.

HUNGER RELIEF AND FOOD SECURITY

No one should have to choose between hunger and survival. We provide emergency food assistance to vulnerable families while promoting sustainable food security initiatives that strengthen household resilience, restore dignity, and improve community wellbeing.

WOMEN’S EMPOWERMENT

Women are powerful agents of change. Through skills development, entrepreneurship support, leadership training, and economic empowerment programs, we equip women with the knowledge, confidence, and opportunities to become financially independent and improve the wellbeing of their families and communities.

PREVENTION OF GENDER BASED VIOLENCE

Every woman and girl has the right to live free from violence, abuse, and discrimination. We promote awareness, prevention, advocacy, and survivor centered support while engaging families and communities to challenge harmful practices and create safer environments where women and girls can thrive.

At DEVADI, we believe lasting change begins when people are empowered with knowledge, protected by justice, and supported with opportunities to build better lives. Through collaboration with communities, volunteers, donors, and development partners, we are restoring hope, protecting dignity, and creating pathways to a more just, inclusive, and prosperous Nigeria. Together, we can give a voice to the unheard, protect the vulnerable, and build communities where every person has the opportunity to thrive.

Our Core Values

What guides every decision we make.

Compassion

We approach every individual with empathy and respect, understanding their unique challenges and treating everyone with dignity.

Equity

We are committed to creating equal opportunities for all, particularly for the most marginalized and vulnerable.

Accountability

We operate with transparency and hold ourselves responsible to our beneficiaries and donors for the impact we create.

Empowerment

We believe in building capacity and self-reliance, not dependency — empowering people to shape their own futures.

Community-Centered

Sustainability

We design programs for lasting change, building systems that create long-term impact and social transformation.

Our Projects

Transforming Lives Through Impact-Driven Programs

JUSTICE BEHIND BARS

We provide legal aid, case reviews, bail advocacy, and reintegration support for unjustly incarcerated and awaiting trial inmates.

HOPE WITHOUT HUNGER

We support vulnerable families with food relief combined with nutrition education, household resilience, and community food security.

GIRLS RISE ABOVE LIMITATIONS

We support vulnerable and indigent girls in school through scholarships, menstrual health support, mentoring, and life skills development.

WOMEN THRIVES

We empower women through vocational training, entrepreneurship support, financial literacy, and access to micro-grants that promote sustainable economic independence.

KNOW YOUR RIGHTS

We provide community legal education through workshops, awareness campaigns, and mobile legal clinics to help individuals understand their rights and access justice. Our goal is to empower underserved communities with the legal knowledge and support they need to protect themselves and seek fair treatment under the law.

Our Commitment

A beacon of hope, a catalyst for change.

Every day, our dedicated team works tirelessly to ensure that vulnerable populations have access to food, education, skills, and protection. We believe that with collective action and unwavering commitment, we can build a more just, equitable, and compassionate society where everyone has the opportunity to reach their full potential.
