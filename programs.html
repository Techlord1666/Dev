How we're making a difference.

Five areas of focus, one shared purpose: giving every person the opportunity to thrive.

01 · Girls Rise Above & Limitations

Scholarships,Health support and Mentoring.

We support vulnerable and indigent girls in school through scholarships, menstrual health support, mentoring, and life skills.

02 · JUSTICE BEHIND BARS

reintegration support for unjustly incarcerated.

We provide legal aid, case reviews, bail advocacy, and reintegration support for unjustly incarcerated and awaiting trial inmates.

03 · Youth Mentorship

Youth development.

We mentor young people with life skills training, vocational guidance, and personal development programs, guiding them toward brighter futures through mentorship, skills training, and career pathways to become leaders and productive members of society.

04 · Women's Empowerment

Economic empowerment.

We empower young women and single mothers through skills training, economic support, business development, and leadership programs — supporting them to build sustainable livelihoods and achieve financial independence.

05 · Gender Justice & Advocacy

Violence prevention.

We actively advocate against gender-based violence through awareness campaigns, survivor support, community engagement, and policy advocacy — creating safer communities through awareness, survivor support, and advocacy for gender justice.
